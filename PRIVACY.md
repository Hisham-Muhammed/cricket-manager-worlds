# Privacy Policy — Cricket Manager Worlds

_Last updated: 13 October 2026_

Cricket Manager Worlds is a persistent multiplayer cricket management
game. This policy explains what data the game collects and how it is
used.

## Data we collect

- **Account data:** your manager name and email address when you
  register with email/password, or your name, email address and profile
  picture when you sign in with Google.
- **Game data:** the worlds you create or join, your club, squad,
  auction bids, tactics, fixtures and results.
- **Session cookie:** a single sign-in cookie that keeps you logged in.

## How the data is used

Data is used only to run the game: signing you in, saving your club
and squad, running auctions and scheduled matches, and showing league
tables, news and notifications to the managers in your world.

We do **not** sell personal data, and we do not use it for advertising.

## Google sign-in

If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and
profile picture from Google and use them only to create and sign in to
your game account. Our use of Google user data follows the
[Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy).

## Storage

Game data is stored in a Supabase (PostgreSQL) database. Passwords —
including world passwords — are stored only as bcrypt hashes and are
never shown in the app or the API.

## Your choices

You can log out at any time. To have your account and game data
deleted, open an issue on this repository and the maintainer will
remove it.
